Astronautics Visualisers

Small interactive tools for the parts of orbital mechanics that are hard to picture from a chalkboard. Each one is a single page — drag the sliders and the maths moves.

Cutting the Cone

Tilt a plane through a double cone and watch the section turn from circle to ellipse to parabola to hyperbola. Shows why an orbit can only ever be one of those four.

What these are

Study aids built alongside MAE 3304 Astronautics I at the University of Texas at Arlington. They are not official course material and carry no endorsement from the department — just an attempt to make a few ideas visible.

Each tool is one self-contained HTML file with no build step. Open it, or clone the repository and open the file directly. Everything runs in the browser.

Course reference

Curtis, H. D. (2020). Orbital Mechanics for Engineering Students, Revised 4th Edition. Butterworth-Heinemann, Elsevier.
